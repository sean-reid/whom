          How to play

          The face is a real person with pages on forty or more Wikimedia sites, born since 1900. Type a first name and press Guess. You get eight.

          After each guess, one line says how your name compares with the answer: how long it is, where it starts in the alphabet, where the people who carry it come from, how it sounds, how common it is, and when it was in fashion. A seventh phrase appears when the two names are forms of one name, or one is short for the other. Matches are in solid ink.

          Earlier guesses sort with the closest match first; your last guess stays by the input. A fact about the person appears after guesses two, four, and six. A new face arrives at midnight, your time.
